## Introduction

ComputerHacking101.com (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have about your data.

_By visiting or using ComputerHacking101.com you agree to the practices described below._

---

## 1. What We Collect

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|----------|----------|---------|
| **Contact data** | Email address submitted via newsletter sign‑up, feedback forms | Respond to you & send updates |
| **Usage data** | IP address, browser type, device info, pages visited, referring URL | Site analytics & security |
| **Cookies** | Small text files in your browser | Remember preferences, measure traffic |

We do **not** knowingly collect sensitive personal information or data from children under 13.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

* **Service delivery** – render pages, remember dark‑mode preference  
* **Communication** – answer your questions, send newsletters you requested  
* **Analytics** – learn which tutorials people read to improve content  
* **Security** – detect spam, fraud, or abuse  
* **Legal compliance** – comply with laws and regulatory obligations

We never sell your personal data.

---

## 3. Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

1. **Consent** – e.g., newsletter opt‑in  
2. **Legitimate Interest** – operate and secure the website  
3. **Legal Obligation** – maintain records where required

## 4. Data Sharing

We share data only with trusted providers who help operate the site:

* **Cloudflare** – CDN, caching, DDoS protection  
* **Hosting/VPS** – serves site assets  
* **Simple Analytics** – privacy‑friendly metrics  
* **Email provider** – send newsletters

All are bound by confidentiality and process data solely on our instructions.

---

## 5. Cookies

| Cookie | Purpose | Lifespan |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `theme` | Stores light/dark mode preference | 1 year |
| `__cf_bm` | Cloudflare bot‑management | 30 minutes |

You can delete or block cookies in your browser. Some features may not work if you disable them.

---

## 6. Retention

* **Emails** – kept until you unsubscribe or 24 months of inactivity  
* **Server logs** – 90 days then deleted  
* **Analytics** – anonymised after 30 days

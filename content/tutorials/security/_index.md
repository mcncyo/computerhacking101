# Security & Hacking

Welcome to the Security & Hacking section, where we explore the world of cybersecurity, ethical hacking, and system hardening. Whether you're a beginner looking to secure your first server or an experienced professional interested in penetration testing, these tutorials will provide practical, hands-on knowledge.

## Why Learn Security?

- Protect your personal data and privacy
- Secure your applications and infrastructure
- Understand how attackers think and operate
- Build a career in the growing field of cybersecurity
- Stay compliant with industry regulations

## Security Best Practices

1. **Keep Systems Updated**: Regularly update your OS and applications
2. **Use Strong Authentication**: Implement MFA wherever possible
3. **Least Privilege**: Only grant necessary permissions
4. **Encrypt Sensitive Data**: Both at rest and in transit
5. **Regular Backups**: Test your backup and recovery procedures
6. **Security Audits**: Regularly assess your security posture
7. **Stay Informed**: Follow security news and updates

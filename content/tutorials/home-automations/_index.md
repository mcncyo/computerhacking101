<h2>FAQ</h2>

**Do I need soldering or firmware flashing skills?**  
No. Every tutorial here uses off-the-shelf devices or TechNest’s upcoming pre-configured hardware.

**Why choose Zigbee over Wi-Fi sensors?**  
Zigbee forms a robust, low-power mesh network—fewer drop-outs, longer battery life and no 2.4 GHz congestion.

**Is Home Assistant beginner-friendly?**  
Absolutely. You can build automations with the visual editor today and dig into YAML only when you want extra power.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Home Assistant?

Home Assistant is an open-source platform that connects and automates devices in your home, giving you full control over your smart environment — all running locally for privacy.

---

### Do I need to know coding to use Home Assistant?

Nope! Many automations can be created visually with the user interface. But if you love digging deeper, you can also write YAML automations for ultimate flexibility.

---

### Can Home Assistant integrate with Zigbee devices?

Absolutely! Home Assistant works beautifully with Zigbee hubs like ConBee, Sonoff Dongle, or SkyConnect, letting you add sensors, switches, and lights seamlessly.

---

### Is Home Assistant secure?

Yes — as long as you follow best practices like:

- Using strong passwords
- Keeping your system updated
- Running Home Assistant behind a secure network
- Avoiding exposing your system directly to the internet without protection
